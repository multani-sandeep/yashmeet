## Session 1 — English · 45 min · Timed Q2 + Q3 Back-to-Back

**Today's focus: combining Language Paper 1 reading questions under time pressure**

### Extract — Read carefully (5 min)

> *The house had been empty for six years before the Okafors bought it, and it showed. Ivy had claimed the east wall entirely, and the front step had sunk at one corner so that rainwater pooled there after every storm, a small dark mirror reflecting whoever stood at the door.*
>
> *Their daughter, Adaeze, was the first to go inside properly — while her parents argued with the estate agent about the damp — and she found that the emptiness had a sound to it, a particular kind of quiet that seemed to hold its breath. Dust moved in the single shaft of light from the landing window like something underwater. She went from room to room without meaning to count them, and somehow arrived back at the front door having done a full circuit, the house rearranging itself around her into somewhere she almost recognised.*
>
> *By the time her parents came in, she was sitting on the stairs, and she told them, with the flat certainty of a much older person, that she liked it. Her mother laughed. Her father did not. He had noticed, without saying so, that his daughter had chosen to sit exactly where the previous family's photographs — still visible as pale rectangles on the wallpaper — suggested a child once had.*

### Task 1 — Q2 Language Analysis, timed (10 min)

> How does the writer use language to describe the empty house? [8 marks]

Identify 2–3 language features, quote, and analyse effect. Keep it tight — this is a speed drill, not a full essay.

### Task 2 — Q3 Structural Analysis, timed (10 min)

> How has the writer structured the text to interest the reader? [8 marks]

Focus on: the shift from parents to Adaeze; what is withheld (the previous family) vs revealed late; the final image.

## Session 2 — Biology · 45 min · Genetics & Evolution (Y10 Preview)

### Part A — Key Definitions (15 min)

Define: **gene · allele · genotype · phenotype · dominant allele · recessive allele**

State **two** differences between mitosis and meiosis (purpose, number of divisions, number/type of cells produced).

### Part B — Punnett Squares (15 min)

Brown eyes (B) are dominant over blue eyes (b).

1. Draw a Punnett square for a cross between two heterozygous parents (Bb × Bb). State the phenotype ratio of offspring.
2. A homozygous recessive parent (bb) is crossed with a heterozygous parent (Bb). Draw the Punnett square and state the phenotype ratio.
3. Two blue-eyed parents have a child. What is the probability the child has brown eyes? Explain.

> (b) Explain why the population became almost entirely resistant after several generations. [3]
