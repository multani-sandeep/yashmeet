## Session 1 — Maths · 45 min · Probability

### Part A — Combined Events (15 min)

- Independent events: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)
- Mutually exclusive events: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)

1. A fair coin is flipped and a fair die is rolled. Calculate P(heads and a 6).
2. A bag has 5 red, 3 blue, 2 green counters. One is drawn at random. Calculate P(red or green).
3. Two fair dice are rolled. Calculate P(both show the same number).

### Part B — Tree Diagrams (15 min)

> A box contains 4 red pens and 6 blue pens. A pen is drawn at random and **not** replaced, then a second pen is drawn.

1. Draw the tree diagram, labelling all branch probabilities.
2. Calculate P(both pens are red).
3. Calculate P(exactly one red pen is drawn).

### Part C — Exam Question: Venn Diagrams (15 min)

> In a class of 30 students, 18 study Spanish, 14 study French, and 7 study both.
>
> (a) Draw a Venn diagram showing this information. [2]
> (b) How many students study neither language? [2]
> (c) A student is picked at random. Calculate the probability they study exactly one of the two languages. [2]

## Session 2 — Physics · 45 min · Thermal Physics (Y10 Preview)

### Part A — Specific Heat Capacity (15 min)

E = mcΔT (energy = mass × specific heat capacity × change in temperature)

1. Calculate the energy needed to heat 2 kg of water (c = 4200 J/kg°C) from 20°C to 80°C.
2. A 0.5 kg block of aluminium (c = 900 J/kg°C) absorbs 18,000 J of energy. Calculate its temperature rise.
3. State what "specific heat capacity" means, in your own words.

### Part B — Ideal Gas Law (15 min)

P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂ (temperature must be in Kelvin: K = °C + 273)

1. A gas has pressure 150 kPa, volume 3 m³, at 27°C. The gas is heated to 127°C at constant volume. Calculate the new pressure.
2. A gas at 200 kPa and 2 m³ is compressed to 0.5 m³ at constant temperature. Calculate the new pressure.

### Part C — Exam Question (15 min)

> A kettle heats 1.5 kg of water from 20°C to 100°C.
>
> (a) Calculate the energy required. (c of water = 4200 J/kg°C) [2]
> (b) The kettle has a power rating of 2000 W. Calculate the minimum time to heat the water. [2]
> (c) Explain, using kinetic theory, why the gas pressure inside a sealed, heated container increases as temperature rises. [3]

### Sources

**Source B** — Official Tsarist government report to Nicholas II, February 1917:
> "The disturbances in the capital are the work of a small number of agitators exploiting temporary supply difficulties. The garrison remains loyal and the situation is fully under Your Majesty's control."
