SESSION 2 - PHYSICS (45 min) - Magnetism and Electromagnetism
---------------------------------------------------------

Part A:
1. Field line direction rule:
Field lines run from the North pole to the South pole outside the
magnet.

2. Solenoid:
A coil of wire; when current flows through it, it produces a
magnetic field similar in shape to a bar magnet's, so it behaves
as an electromagnet.

3. Advantage of electromagnet over permanent magnet:
Its magnetic field can be turned on/off and its strength varied
(by changing the current), unlike a permanent magnet.

Part B - Motor effect:

1. Force (working):
F = B x I x L = 0.5 x 3 x 0.4
    Answer = 0.6 N

2. Current (working):
I = F / (B x L) = 0.6 / (0.8 x 0.5)
    Answer = 1.5 A

3. Fleming's Left-Hand Rule:
Thumb = Force (motion), First finger = Field, Second finger =
Current. Point first finger along the field direction and second
finger along the current direction; the thumb then points in the
direction of the force.

Part C - Exam question (transformer, 200:1000 turns, Vp = 24V):

(a) Secondary voltage (working):
Vs = Vp x (Ns/Np) = 24 x (1000/200)
    Answer = 120 V

(b) Step-up or step-down:
    Answer = Step-up transformer, because the secondary coil has
    more turns (1000) than the primary coil (200), so the voltage
    is increased.

(c) How energy transfers (electromagnetic induction):
The alternating current in the primary coil creates a changing
magnetic field. This changing field passes through the iron core
to the secondary coil, and the changing magnetic field induces an
alternating voltage in the secondary coil - transferring energy via
the magnetic field rather than a direct electrical connection.

SECONDARY - COMPUTING (30 min) - Networks
---------------------------------------------------------

Part A:
1. LAN vs WAN:
LAN covers a small area (e.g. one building/site); WAN covers a
large geographic area and connects multiple LANs (the internet is
the largest WAN).

2. Matching:
Router = directs data packets between different networks
Switch  = connects devices within a single LAN
NIC     = allows a device to connect to a network

Part B - Topologies:
Star  - advantage:                       disadvantage:
Ring  - advantage:                       disadvantage:

(Suggested answers: Star - advantage: one device failing doesn't
affect others; disadvantage: if the central switch fails, the whole
network goes down. Ring - advantage: organised, controlled data
flow; disadvantage: one broken connection/device can disrupt the
whole network.)

Part C - Exam question:

(a) Role of DNS server:
Translates the human-readable domain name typed into the browser
into the numerical IP address needed to locate and connect to the
correct server.

(b) Packet switching:
Data is broken into small packets, each sent independently (often
via different routes) across the network, then reassembled in the
correct order at the destination. It is used because it makes
efficient use of network capacity, allows many users to share the
network at once, and is resilient if one route fails.
