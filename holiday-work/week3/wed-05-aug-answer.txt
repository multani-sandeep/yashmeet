Part C - Exam question (antibiotic resistance):

(a) Why some bacteria survived:
Variation existed in the population - a small number of bacteria
already had a mutation giving resistance; these survived the
antibiotic treatment while non-resistant bacteria died.

(b) Why population became almost entirely resistant:
Surviving resistant bacteria reproduced, passing the resistance
allele to offspring. Over generations, the resistant bacteria
out-competed non-resistant ones, so the resistance allele became
much more frequent in the population.

(c) Why overuse makes it worse:
More frequent/widespread antibiotic use increases the selection
pressure and the number of opportunities for resistant strains to
be selected for and spread (and incomplete courses can leave
partially-resistant bacteria alive to reproduce).

1. Stage 2 pyramid shape:

death rate) - a triangular shape. Example: Niger / Mali.

2. Stage 4/5 pyramid shape:
Narrower base (low birth rate), roughly even column shape with a
narrowing only near the very top (low death rate, people live to
old age). Example: UK / Japan.
