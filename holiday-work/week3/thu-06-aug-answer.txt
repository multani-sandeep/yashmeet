Part A - Combined events (working):

    Answer = 1/12

    Answer = 7/10

    Answer = 1/6

Part B - Tree diagram (4 red, 6 blue, no replacement):

1. Branch probabilities:
1st draw: P(Red) = 4/10,  P(Blue) = 6/10
2nd draw if 1st was Red:  P(Red) = 3/9, P(Blue) = 6/9
2nd draw if 1st was Blue: P(Red) = 4/9, P(Blue) = 5/9

2. P(both red) (working):
4/10 x 3/9 = 12/90
    Answer = 2/15

3. P(exactly one red) (working):
(4/10 x 6/9) + (6/10 x 4/9) = 24/90 + 24/90 = 48/90
    Answer = 8/15

Part C - Venn diagram (30 students, Spanish=18, French=14, both=7):

(a) Venn diagram values: Spanish only = 11, French only = 7, both = 7

(b) Neither language (working):
30 - (11 + 7 + 7)
    Answer = 5 students

(c) P(exactly one language) (working):
(11 + 7) / 30 = 18/30
    Answer = 3/5 (0.6)

Part A - Specific heat capacity:

1. Energy to heat water (working):
E = 2 x 4200 x (80-20)
    Answer = 504,000 J (504 kJ)

2. Temperature rise of aluminium (working):
deltaT = 18000 / (0.5 x 900)
    Answer = 40 degrees C

3. Definition:
Specific heat capacity is the energy needed to raise the
temperature of 1 kg of a substance by 1 degree C (or 1 K).

Part B - Ideal gas law:

1. New pressure (constant volume) (working):
T1 = 300 K, T2 = 400 K
P2 = 150 x 400 / 300
    Answer = 200 kPa

2. New pressure (constant temperature) (working):
P2 = 200 x 2 / 0.5
    Answer = 800 kPa

Part C - Exam question:

(a) Energy required (working):
E = 1.5 x 4200 x (100-20)
    Answer = 504,000 J (504 kJ)

(b) Minimum time (working):
t = 504000 / 2000
    Answer = 252 seconds (4.2 minutes)

(c) Kinetic theory explanation:
As temperature rises, gas molecules gain kinetic energy and move
faster. They collide with the container walls more frequently and
with greater force. Since pressure comes from these collisions,
more frequent/forceful collisions increase the pressure (volume
and amount of gas being constant).
