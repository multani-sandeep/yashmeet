Binary Basics - Convert to denary:
1. 0000 1010 = 10

3. 1000 1111 = 143
4. 1111 1111 = 255

5. 25  = 00011001

Bytes and Units - complete:
- 8 bits = 1 _byte__
- 1,024 bytes = 1 _kilobyte__
- 1,024 KB = 1 _megabyte__
- 1,024 MB = 1 _gigabyte__
