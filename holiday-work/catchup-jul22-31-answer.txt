BIOLOGY - Respiration and Excretion
---------------------------------------------------------

1. Two differences (aerobic vs anaerobic):
Aerobic needs oxygen; anaerobic does not.
Aerobic produces much more ATP/energy than anaerobic.

2. Why lactic acid builds up and heavy breathing continues:
During intense exercise, oxygen supply cannot meet demand, so
muscles respire anaerobically, producing lactic acid (oxygen debt).
After exercise, extra oxygen is needed to break down (oxidise) the
lactic acid that built up - this is why breathing stays heavy.

3. Nephron sequence:
Filtration (in the glomerulus, under pressure - small molecules:
water, glucose, urea, ions pass into the tubule) -> selective
reabsorption (all glucose, most water, some ions reabsorbed back
into the blood) -> remaining fluid (urea, excess water/ions)
continues as urine.
